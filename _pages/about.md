I am a researcher in empirical corporate finance and empirical asset pricing. My current work studies how financial contracting, corporate governance, and managerial background shape firm outcomes, with a particular interest in workplace safety, creditor rights, and the cost of capital.

I am a first-year PhD student in the School of Accounting and Finance at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU).

Research Interests
======

* Empirical Corporate Finance
* Empirical Asset Pricing
* Corporate Governance
* Financial Contracting and Bankruptcy
* Workplace Safety and Employee Welfare
